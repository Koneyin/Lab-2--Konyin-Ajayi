The Best Cinnamon Rolls!

Description

Soft, warm, yummy, packed with cinnamon, and ready in ~2 hours 30 minutes. What more could entice you? Perfect to pair with a glass of hot chocolate, or, just by themselves because they're just as good.

Ingredients

For the dough:

  • ¾ cup milk
  • 2¼ teaspoons quick rise or active dry yeast
  • ¼ cup of granulated sugar
  • 4 tablespoons of salted butter, melted
  • 1 large egg and 1 large egg yolk, both at room temperature
  • 3 cups of bread flour, can add more as needed
  • ¾ teaspoon of salt
  • Extra-virgin olive oil to lightly grease the pan

For the cinnamon filling:

  • 4 tablespoons of softened, salted butter
  • ⅔ cup of packed dark brown sugar
  • 1½ tablespoons of ground cinnamon

For the cream cheese frosting:

  • 4 oz of softened cream cheese
  • ¾ cup of powdered sugar
  • 3 tablespoons of softened salted butter
  • ½ teaspoon of vanilla extract

Preparation

  1. In a microwave safe bowl, heat up the milk by microwaving for 45 seconds to 1 minute (depending on the power of ur microwave) or until it reaches roughly 115°F on your cooking thermometer; or until it feels like warm bath water to the touch.

  2. Pour the warmed milk into larger bowl (or a stand mixer if you have one) and sprinkle the yeast on top. If using active dry yeast, add the sugar now and let the yeast sit with the sugar for roughly 5 minutes until foamy. If not, then add the sugar, followed by the melted butter, egg, and egg yolk in that order. Mix well (or on medium speed if using a stand mixer) until everything is well combined. Then use a wooden/silicone spoon to gently mix in the flour and salt until the dough starts to take form.

  3. Mix the dough on a generously-floured surface if using your hands (8 to 10 minutes). If using a stand mixer, mix on medium speed until it comes together and feels soft and a little sticky (8 to 10 minutes). If it's too sticky (constantly sticking to everything) add another 1 to 3 tablespoons of bread flour, depending on how sticky it is. In the meantime, place a clean dish towel in your dryer to warm up.

  4. Lightly oil a large bowl and transfer the dough into it. Cover it with plastic wrap and the warm towel from the dryer. Allow the dough to rise until it roughly doubles in size (1 to 1½ hours). You will know the dough is ready when you can poke your finger about an inch into it and without the dent immediately bouncing back. Generously dust a surface with flour, then place the dough on it and use a rolling pin to flatten it to roughly a 10 x 14 inch rectangle.

  5. To prepare the cinnamon filling, spread the softened butter over the dough, leaving a ¼-inch margin at the far short side of the rectangle. In a small bowl, mix together the brown sugar and cinnamon. After, use your hands to sprinkle as little or as much sugary goodness as you want over the butter, then rub the mix into the butter.

  6. Starting from the short side without the margin, tightly roll up your dough and seal the far edge of it by pinching the dough together. Now start cutting the dough with the seam facing down. You can optionally cut off a ½ inch off the ends of each side of the dough, as they likely won't have enough filling, but it's completely optional. Give the dough log a firm but gentle push inwards on both ends so that it compacts slightly, as it may have stretched out a bit wduring the rolling process.

  7. Using a serrated knife or unflavoured dental floss, cut the dough into about nine 1-inch rolls. Line a round or square 9 inch pan with parchment paper and place the rolls inside. Cover with plastic wrap and a towel and let it rise again until the rolls have puffed up and have about doubled in size and are touching eachother inside the pan (45 minutes to 1 hour). Meanwhile, preheat your oven to 350°F.

  8. Remove the plastic wrap and towel once the dough has risen enough, and bake them for 20 to 25 minutes until they're golden brown on the edges. Ensure to not overbake the rolls so that they stay soft in the middle. Let them cool for 10 minutes once taken out of the oven. In the meantime, prepare the cream cheese frosting by combining the cream cheese, powdered sugar, butter, and vanilla extract in a bowl and beat well. If using a stand mixer, beat on low speed for 30 seconds, then increase to medium-high speed until you reach your preferred consistency of smooth and fluffy. Spread the frosting over the warm rolls and serve.

Congratulations! You've just made the best fluffy, warm, and yummy cinnamon rolls to share with family, friends, or maybe not share at all. Cinnamon roll image is from Flickr Photos.
